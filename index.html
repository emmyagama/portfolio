B1 — Grounded Q&A on Nigerian Health Regulations

B1 is a plain-language question-answering assistant for Nigerian health-sector regulation, built for diagnostic-centre managers and facility operators who currently sift through eight long PDFs from six different regulators to answer basic compliance questions. It answers only from those source documents, names the document and section behind every claim, and refuses rather than guesses when it doesn't know. On a 30-question evaluation set — 25 answerable, 5 deliberately unanswerable — it produced 0 fabricated answers on the 5 questions the documents couldn't answer, including a clinical-treatment question it correctly blocked instead of inventing unsafe advice.
